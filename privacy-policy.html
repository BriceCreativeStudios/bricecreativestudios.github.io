Compound Survival Privacy Policy

Developer: Brice Creative Studios · South Australia, Australia
Last updated: 8 November 2026

Brice Creative Studios respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Android game Compound Survival handles information, based on the game version reviewed before release. We will update this policy if the released version handles information differently.

Information we collect

Compound Survival is designed to run without a player account. In the inspected version, we did not identify collection of personal information, device identifiers, location, gameplay analytics or financial information by Brice Creative Studios. We did not identify any transfer of player information to our servers or third parties.

Game saves and settings

The game stores campaign progress, game settings, audio preferences and tutorial or presentation preferences locally on your device. These files support the game's functionality. They are not sent to Brice Creative Studios by the inspected version.

Internet access and third-party services

The inspected Android application did not request internet access and no in-game network, advertising, analytics, cloud-save, crash-reporting or purchase integration was identified. The game uses the Godot engine and Android runtime libraries. Device operating-system and app-store services may operate independently of the game under their own privacy terms.

Sharing of information

We did not identify any in-game sharing of personal information with advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties in the inspected version.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device unless removed through the game's or device's available controls. Uninstalling the game generally removes its app-local files, but Android device backup or restore features may affect whether some settings or progress can be restored. Brice Creative Studios does not operate an account or cloud-save service for the inspected version.

Children's privacy

The inspected version does not provide accounts, advertising or identified personal-data collection features. Parents and guardians can manage device and app-store settings through the relevant platform.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Compound Survival's features or data handling change. We will publish the updated policy here with a revised date.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning Compound Survival, contact Brice Creative Studios at: coreybrice@y7mail.com.
