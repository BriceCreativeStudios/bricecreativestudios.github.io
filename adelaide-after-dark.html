Adelaide After Dark

A vampire survival RPG in development, set against a tactical map of Adelaide. Explore the city’s surface and fictional underground enclaves, manage local attention and seek shelter before sunrise.

Running desktop gameplay in portrait format, using staged demonstration states. In-panel environments and characters include original AI-generated fictional illustrations. Underground enclaves are fictional, not verified real Adelaide tunnels or access advice.

The current prototype

Version 0.9 introduces The First Night: a playable introductory chapter with clan representatives, persistent conversations, branching choices and illustrated action outcomes. Build your standing within a district clan. Designed for Android portrait play; finished voice acting and complete long-term campaigns are not claimed.
